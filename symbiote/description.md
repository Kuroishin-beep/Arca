# Arca

Your Dragonbane table's shared inventory and character sheets, inside TaleSpire.

- **Inventory:** every pack, cart and chest the party owns. Changes show up live for everyone at the table.
- **Character sheets:** laid out like the printed Dragonbane sheet, and editable during play.
- **Rolls:** the d20 next to each skill and attribute, and the damage dice next to each weapon, go straight to TaleSpire's dice tray.

Sign in with the email your GM added to the campaign.
